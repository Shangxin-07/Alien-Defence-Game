# Tech tree: today, hybrid and alien

Proposal from 2026-11-04, after Mike asked for "a proper set of techs, from normal today techs, to
slightly better hybrid techs, to fully advanced alien techs, along with the levels and places to
find, research or get them." **Nothing here is built.** Numbers are placeholders to tune in play.
Mike made the open choices on 2026-11-04; see [Decisions](#decisions).

## Rules this has to keep

- **The aliens stay ahead.** Their gear climbs on its own clock, and faster when you field better
  kit (see [The aliens climb too](#the-aliens-climb-too)). You close the gap; you never pass them.
  Their own guns never overheat, whatever you research.
- **Harder, never impossible.** Every tier has more than one way in. Nothing depends on one
  person surviving: knowledge is written down, and a dead researcher loses no finished work.
- **No free lunches.** Better kit costs something you are always short of: alien power, alloy,
  money, manpower, or risk on the way to get it.
- **Today's tech needs no research to use.** Everyone knows how to fire a rifle. Research and the
  workshop are for alien things, and for making what you can't find.
- **Defaults are never eased.** None of this makes the current game easier. Kit only gets better
  when it's earned.

## Three families, nine levels

| Level | Family | What it is | Typical way in |
|---|---|---|---|
| **T1 Street** | Today | What a city leaves lying about: pistols, shotguns, hunting rifles, vests, batons, pipe bombs. | Homes, police stations, hardware stores, the black market. Where the game starts. |
| **T2 Surplus** | Today | Police and army issue: assault rifles, body armour, grenades, night-vision, radios. | Police stations (thin), army depots (new place, dangerous), soldiers who join, the black market. |
| **T3 Front line** | Today | Heavy army kit: rocket launchers, demolition charges, ceramic plate, motion trackers, armoured vehicles. | Bought from the blocs once a government is talking to you, or the bottom of an army depot. |
| **H1 Alloy-worked** | Hybrid | Our things made better with alien hull alloy: alloy-plated armour, alloy-tipped rounds, alloy shields. | Research alloys, then work salvaged alloy in a workshop. |
| **H2 Cell-fed** | Hybrid | Our designs running on alien power cells: cell carbine, heartbeat scanner, regen medikit, shaped plasma charge. | Research plus captured cells, which are always short. |
| **H3 Integrated** | Hybrid | Alien parts built into whole systems: powered alloy suits, squad link headsets, the cell truck. | Long research chains, a craft core studied, a workshop with a lift. |
| **A1 Captured** | Alien | Alien guns and kit used as they come. Hot in our hands and on a draining cell. | Taken off the dead and the captured, once studied. (Plasma pistol, scattergun and needle rifle are built today.) |
| **A2 Understood** | Alien | We learn to vent, recharge and copy them. | Deep research, a live captive of the right race, and a craft core to recharge from. |
| **A3 Mastered** | Alien | Their heavy weapons, their armour, their flight. Still not quite as they use them. | Only from late targets: landed craft, alien bases, commanders. |

The families overlap in time. A good mid-game squad carries T2 rifles with alloy rounds (H1),
one captured plasma gun (A1) and a heartbeat scanner (H2).

## The scales the tiers climb

Every tier should read as a clear step on the numbers the fights already use.

### Breach (what a weapon can break)

Today: 0 flimsy, 1 solid, 2 masonry. Two more levels are added:

| Breach | Breaks | Today's weapons | New weapons that reach it |
|---|---|---|---|
| 0 | glass, crates, bins | service pistol | T1 pistols, smoke |
| 1 | doors, trees, cars | rifle, shotgun, needle rifle | T1/T2 long guns, alloy-tipped pistol rounds (H1) |
| 2 | brick, concrete | plasma pistol, plasma scattergun | frag grenade, pipe bomb (T1/T2), alloy-tipped rifle rounds (H1), cell carbine (H2) |
| **3 reinforced** | bunkers, vault doors, your own base walls, craft bulkheads | none | rocket launcher, demolition charge (T3), alien grenade (A1), alien siege caster (A3) |
| **4 hull** | alien craft outer hull | none | shaped plasma charge (H2), lance (A3) |

So a landed craft can't be entered through its side until you have H2 or A3: you go in through
the hatch, where they're waiting.

### Armour and power

| | Today | Hybrid | Alien |
|---|---|---|---|
| Armour worn | jacket 2, stab vest 4, body armour 8, ceramic plate 12 (slows you) | alloy vest 12, alloy plate 16, powered suit 22 | their hide/shell suits 18–26, but only when understood (A2+) |
| Gun power | pistol 26, rifle 30, shotgun 44, LMG 32, marksman 38, rocket 90 | alloy rounds +6, cell carbine 46 | plasma 48–52 now, siege caster 70, lance 85 |
| Alien armour they face | Shade 3, Strider 6, Brute 12 today, rising +4 to +6 per alien tech level |

Rifle rounds (30) against a Brute in its second-level armour (18) do 12 on an average hit. That's
the pressure that pushes you up the tree.

## What you need to make things

Existing: **scrap**, **money**, **fuel**, **medicine**. New:

| Material | What it is | Where it comes from | Can we make it? |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Chemicals** | Fertiliser, solvents, propellant | Hardware stores, industrial estates, pharmacies (small) | No. Scavenged or bought. |
| **Alien alloy** | Hull fragments and armour plates | Crash sites, landed craft, Brute corpses (plates), alien bases | No. Only salvaged. |
| **Power cells** | The charge pack in every alien gun | One in each alien gun picked up, sometimes loose on a body | Recharged at A2 from a core. Late research (A3) recharges a spent cell to a third without a core. |
| **Craft cores** | What powers their ships | Downed or landed craft only, often damaged in the crash | Never made. Late research (A3) brings a spent core back to a third. |

Cores are the late-game currency, like money is the mid-game one. They power cell recharging,
H3 kit, alien weapon copies and any craft of your own. Every assault on a landed craft is a fight
for one.

## Ammo

Four kinds, and every bullet gun takes one of them. Which gun takes which is on its card, so
nobody needs to know a calibre.

| Kind | Fits | Loads |
|---|---|---|
| **Handgun rounds** | Pistols | Standard (T1), armour-piercing (T2), alloy-tipped (H1) |
| **Rifle rounds** | Assault, hunting and marksman rifles, the LMG | Standard (T1), armour-piercing (T2), alloy-tipped (H1) |
| **Shotgun shells** | Shotguns | Buckshot (T1), slug (T1), alloy flechette (H1) |
| **Rockets** | Rocket launcher | High explosive (T3), anti-armour (T3) |

What a load does, always one clear trade:

| Load | Does | Costs |
|---|---|---|
| Standard | The gun's own numbers | — |
| Armour-piercing | Target's armour counts half | −4 power, never breaks anything more than standard |
| Buckshot | +20% to hit within 4 tiles; can hit the tile next to the target | Falls off hard past 5 tiles, breach 0 |
| Slug | One heavy hit: +8 power, range +4, breach 1 | No spread bonus |
| Alloy-tipped / flechette | +6 power, +1 breach | Costs alloy to make |
| High explosive rocket | Blast radius 2, breach 3 | Hits your own if they're close |
| Anti-armour rocket | Target's armour counts a quarter, power 110 | Blast radius 0, breach 3 on the tile hit only |

Rules:
- A clip (or a box of shells, or one rocket) holds one load. Swapping load in a fight is a normal
  reload. The loadout request says which load each person wants, with standard as the fallback.
- Police stations stock handgun rounds and shells; army depots rifle rounds and armour-piercing;
  rockets come from the blocs. Standard rounds can be reloaded on the bench from scrap and
  chemicals; armour-piercing needs a workshop.
- Grenades and charges are their own items, not ammo.
- Today's single "spare clips" supply splits into these kinds when this is built. Old saves share
  out their clips by the guns in the stores.

Already built: the plasma pistol, plasma scattergun and needle rifle, each researched before use,

- **Each captured gun's cell drains.** 30 shots, then it needs a new cell. Cells come from other

- **Alien grenade:** power 70, radius 2, breach 3. Shades and Strider leaders carry them at alien

- **Their armour can't be worn.** Shells are grown to fit; until A2 they're only alloy to work.

## Research

The current system (one project at a time, person-days of Science, labs +50%, `items` and
`after` as "any one of") stays. What changes:

- **Kinds of project.** *Study* (learn to use it; the item is kept), *reverse-engineer* (learn to
  make it; **one item is used up**), *theory* (no item, needs other projects), *autopsy* and
  *interrogation* (as now).
- **"All of" prerequisites** as well as "any of", so a cell carbine can need both the plasma
  pistol and alloys.
- **Size by family**: today's projects (bench crafts) 6–15 points, hybrid 30–80, alien 100–200.
  A team of three with a lab does about 5 points a day, so an alien project is a month or more.
- **Deep projects need a working lab** (hybrid and up), and alien projects need two.

The existing projects slot straight in: *Survey the crash site* and *Alien alloys* open H1;
*Plasma pistol* and friends are A1 studies; *Tracking implant* opens the heartbeat scanner;
*Hunter biology* opens the regen medikit; the commander projects open the link headset.

## Manufacturing

There is none today. Proposed:

- **The bench**, at any base, no room needed: T1 and T2 crafts (pipe bombs, grenades, reloading
  clips from scrap). Anyone on "construction" can do it at their Engineering pace.
- **The workshop** (the existing room): needed for hybrid and alien work. Each workshop takes up to
  4 people. Jobs cost person-days, materials and sometimes money, and queue like building.
- **Failures.** Hybrid items have a 5% chance of a fault (a cell carbine that won't fire this
  fight, a vest plate that cracks), falling to 1% after a follow-up "refinement" project.

## The aliens climb too

Alien tech level starts at 1 and rises whatever you do:

| Level | Roughly when | What changes |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Start | Today's races and guns |
| 2 | Month 2–3 | Armour +4, alien grenades, Strider leaders carry a heartbeat sense at 16 tiles |
| 3 | Month 5–6 | Armour +6 more, siege casters in Brute hands, the first shield drones |
| 4 | Month 9+ | Lances, craft that don't crash when hit |

On top of the clock, **fielding better kit brings it forward**: when most of a squad carries
hybrid or alien guns, the next level comes up to a month sooner. This is the same idea as the
adaptive aliens (they learn your tactics); here they learn your tools. The player sees it through
hints in battle reports, not a number.

## How it fits the code (for later)

- `GEAR`, `GUNS` and `WEAPONS` grow; `Weapon` gets `cell` (shots per cell) and an ammo
  kind; clips in the stores are counted per kind and load.
- `Project` gets `allOf`, `kind` and `consumes`; `blocker` checks them.
- Terrain gets toughness 3 and 4 for reinforced walls and hull.
- New `Supply` kinds `chemicals`, `alloy`, `cells`, `cores`; a new place kind `depot`.
- A new `manufacture.ts` in `campaign/`, beside `research` in `staff.ts`, and a Workshop panel.
- Blocs (in `geoscape/blocs.ts`) get a sales list once contact is made.

## Decisions

Mike's answers, 2026-11-04:

1. **Ammo stays game-simple: four kinds, a few loads with an obvious purpose.** No real-world
   calibres. Mike, after a second thought: shotgun shells are fine, and so are loads like
   armour-piercing vs high explosive, or buckshot vs slug. Detail as set out in [Ammo](#ammo).
2. **Cells recharge from cores at A2, and later we work some of it out.** At A3 a spent cell (or a
   spent core) can be brought back to a third without a fresh core. Cores are still never made.
3. **Reverse-engineering to make something uses one up.** Studying it to use it doesn't.
4. **Captured guns drain a 30-shot cell** as well as overheating, until recharging (2).
5. **Alien escalation: clock plus response to your kit.** The clock's speed will need tuning.
6. **Blocs sell T3 kit**, each bloc a different list, for money and standing.
7. **Hybrid faults:** 5%, dropping to 1% after a refinement project.
